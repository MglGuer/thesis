\begin{abstract}
Effective social communication is vital for personal and professional interactions, yet many individuals struggle with social engagement due to anxiety, neurodivergence, or difficulty interpreting social cues. Furthermore, existing chatbot technologies often lack emotional intelligence, contextual awareness, and the ability to facilitate meaningful, adaptive interactions. Without these capabilities, chatbots fail to effectively support individuals who need assistance in improving their social communication skills. In this paper, we aim to develop a one on one conversational chatbot to facilitate one-on-one social communication training through social situation emulation, wherein the bot emulates a real person, focused on providing support for young adults aged 18 - 24. Moreover, by identifying individuals who perceive themselves as socially awkward, this chatbot will provide personalized support and guidance to help them navigate social interactions more effectively.
